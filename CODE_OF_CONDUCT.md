# Code of conduct

AriCodeOS is built by people who want to understand and make an operating
system, and it welcomes anyone who shares that interest, whatever their
experience, background or language.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors are not native English
  speakers and many are learning; questions are welcome.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits, with evidence: a log, a test, a
  measurement, a reference to the specification or to an ADR.
- Report problems honestly, including your own mistakes. Failures are not
  hidden here; they are recorded and fixed.
- Credit other people's work, in this project and outside it.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues,
  reviews, commits or any project space.
- Presenting someone else's work, or this project's, as your own.
- Deliberately introducing security weaknesses, back doors or data
  collection.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through the contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled privately. Depending on the
case, the response ranges from a private warning to removal from the
project's spaces.
